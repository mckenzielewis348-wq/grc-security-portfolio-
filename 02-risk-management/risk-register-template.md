## Scoring Guidance
- **Likelihood**: 1 (Rare) to 5 (Almost Certain)
- **Impact**: 1 (Insignificant) to 5 (Severe)
- **Inherent Risk Score**: Likelihood × Impact before controls
- **Residual Risk Score**: Risk level after existing controls
