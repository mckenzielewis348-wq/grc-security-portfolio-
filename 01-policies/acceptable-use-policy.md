# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines acceptable use of organizational information systems, data, and technology resources.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, and third parties with access to organizational systems or data.

## Policy Statements

1. **Authorized Use Only**  
   Users must use organizational systems only for approved business purposes.

2. **Account Responsibility**  
   Users are responsible for all actions performed under their accounts and must protect credentials.

3. **Data Handling**  
   Sensitive data must be accessed, shared, and stored according to data classification and protection requirements.

4. **Prohibited Activities**  
   Users must not:
   - Access systems or data without authorization.
   - Install unapproved software.
   - Circumvent security controls.
   - Use resources for unlawful, abusive, or disruptive purposes.

5. **Monitoring and Privacy**  
   Organizational systems may be monitored to protect security and ensure compliance.

6. **Incident Reporting**  
   Suspected security incidents, misuse, or policy violations must be reported promptly through approved channels.

## Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of access or employment, and potential legal action.

## Review
This policy should be reviewed at least annually and updated as needed.
